Instance Masks + Sparse Keypoints

  • 78,091 tracked shape records across 4,881 frames (30 FPS)
  • Mask-constrained keypoint association eliminates cross-animal label swaps
  • LabelMe-compatible JSON + RLE CSV contracts stay editable at every frame

Human-in-the-Loop Track QC

Surface occlusions and swaps before computing statistics

During high-overlap mounting bouts, single-pass trackers can briefly drop an occluded animal. Annolid's Track QC automatically ranks the highest-risk windows so researchers repair only the frames that matter.

  • Flags the exact 5-frame intruder occlusion gap (frames 3361–3366) during mounting
  • Prioritized review queue scores velocity spikes, area jumps, and missing instances
  • Double-click any alert in Analysis Studio to jump the canvas straight to that frame
annolid-run repair-tracks ./mouse083_task1_annotator1/ --expected-instances 2

Ethograms, Kinematics & Social Proximity

Align behavioral bouts with physical trajectories

Combine frame-level behavior flags or BORIS event logs with calibrated speed, freezing detection, inter-animal distance, and 54 automatically detected anatomical body-part contact bouts.

  • investigation: 25 bouts (39.1 s · 24.0%) · mount: 7 bouts (19.9 s · 12.2%)
  • Inter-mouse proximity spans 15.7 px (contact) to 694.8 px with clear thigmotaxis
  • Includes Inter-Rater Reliability (Cohen's & Fleiss' kappa) and cohort hypothesis testing
annolid-run kinematics mouse083_task1_annotator1_tracked.csv --fps 30 --output-dir ./kin_out

Spatial Zone & Place-Preference Analysis

Draw reusable zones once and score across cohorts

Author polygon, rectangle, or circle zones in Video Tools → Zones with explicit chamber/phase metadata, then export occupancy, entry counts, first-entry latency, and social proximity metrics.

  • right_port_zone: resident 72.5 s (15×), intruder 66.4 s (12×)
  • left_nest_zone: resident 63.4 s (12×), intruder 61.2 s (13×)
  • Supports 3×3 presets, neutral transit connectors, and zone-occupancy audit policies
annolid-run zone-analysis mouse083_task1_annotator1_tracked.csv --zone-file mouse083_zones.json --fps 30

Self-Contained HTML5 + Inline SVG

Share publication-ready analysis reports in one click

Run Analysis → Analysis & Reporting Studio or annolid-run report to bundle Track QC, Kinematics, Spatial Zones, Body-Part Ethograms, and Time Budgets into a single offline HTML5 file.

  • Deterministic inline SVG trajectories, activity budgets, zone bars, and ethogram rasters
  • Simultaneously emits analysis_report.html, .md, and versioned .json
  • Zero external CDN dependencies—email or archive alongside raw video data
annolid-run report mouse083_task1_annotator1_tracked.csv --events-csv events.csv --zone-file zones.json --fps 30

Hierarchical Instance Body-Part Segmentation

Sub-divide tracked animals into anatomical regions with zero bleed

Decompose already-tracked whole-animal polygons into non-overlapping head, torso, and tail masks strictly inside each parent ROI—even during close huddling and coiled postures.

  • Supports seeded, keypoint_guided, and zero-supervision unsupervised modes
  • CPU-fast Lab/gradient backend (~1–3 ms/crop) plus optional DINOv3 hybrid features
  • Feeds directly into annolid-run body-part-ethogram for directed contact scoring
annolid-run segment-body-parts social_assay.mp4 --mode auto --backbone fast --export-csv
